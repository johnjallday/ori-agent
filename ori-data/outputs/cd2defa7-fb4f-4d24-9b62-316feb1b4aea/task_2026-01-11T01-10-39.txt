Here are three great things to do in NYC during winter:

- Ice skate at Bryant Park Winter Village (outdoor)
  - Enjoy skating under the city lights, with a festive market and plenty of food stands nearby. Tip: arrive early to avoid lines for rentals, and you can pair it with a stroll through the nearby market.

- Indoor cultural day at a top museum (The Met, MoMA, or American Museum of Natural History)
  - Escape the chill while exploring world-class collections. Plan 2–3 hours and grab a quick bite at the museum cafe or nearby options.

- Evening entertainment: Broadway show or Lincoln Center performance (indoor)
  - Experience world-class theater or live music/dance. Tip: book tickets in advance and check for any winter-season specials or bundles.
